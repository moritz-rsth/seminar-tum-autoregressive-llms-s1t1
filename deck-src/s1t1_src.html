ⓘ how is the position vector computed?
\begin{aligned} PE_{(pos,\,2i)} &= \sin\!\big(pos / 10000^{2i/d}\big) \\ PE_{(pos,\,2i+1)} &= \cos\!\big(pos / 10000^{2i/d}\big) \end{aligned}

Each pair of dimensions (2i,\,2i{+}1) is a sine/cosine wave with its own frequency, from fast to slow across the d dimensions. Together they give every position pos its own unique pattern.

What would we humans do while we read the words in the correct order?

Context matters

Attention

q_i = x_i \textcolor{#e0218a}{W_Q}, \qquad k_i = x_i \textcolor{#e0218a}{W_K}, \qquad v_i = x_i \textcolor{#e0218a}{W_V} new trainable weights
\mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\surd\overline{d_k}} + M\right) V
Q looking for K contains V passes on M only look back
M = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\infty & -\infty & -\infty \\ 0 & 0 & -\infty & -\infty \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & -\infty \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}

-\infty above the diagonal → e^{-\infty} = 0: no word attends to a later word

Softmax

\mathrm{softmax}(z)_j = e^{z_j} \,/\, \textstyle\sum_k e^{z_k}

how much token i attends to j

\Delta x^{(h)}_i = \Big(\textstyle\sum_{j \le i} a^{(h)}_{i,j}\, v^{(h)}_j\Big)\, \textcolor{#e0218a}{W^{(h)}_O} new trainable weights
